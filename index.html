iii.   Approach

How I
think.

01
Luxury requires its own logic

Standard supply chain templates weren't built for seasonal planning cycles, limited edition drops, or multi-brand portfolio constraints. I configure for how luxury actually operates.

02
The C-suite workshop informs the config

Planning transformation fails when platform setup is disconnected from strategy. I run both — the executive alignment session and the technical configuration that follows from it.

03
Data architecture before AI features

AI-powered demand sensing is only as good as the master data behind it. I build the SAP integration frameworks and data governance that make intelligent planning actually intelligent.

04
Scale is in the design, not the rollout

Configuring for 102 markets doesn't mean 102 separate configurations. The planning architecture has to carry that complexity from the start.

05
Fashion background, technology delivery

NIFT and Ralph Lauren gave me fluency in how fashion businesses think. Accenture and EY gave me the delivery method. Together they close the translation gap that most implementation teams miss.
